# The Validation · FinWise

> Module 5 · Experimentation Methods. The experiment brief that tests the bet.

## Method

_A/B Test:  One user's onboarding doesn't affect another's, you have two clear arms, and you can reach significance in 2-3 weeks at reasonable trial volume.

_____

## Hypothesis + primary metric

_IF we introduce a weekly financial check-in with a visible streak counter (loss-aversion framing) THEN sessions-per-user and financial-modeling feature usage WILL increase within 6 weeks BECAUSE recurring, cadence-based behavior responds to loss-aversion mechanics better than one-time completion mechanics, which is what's missing for customers 2+ months post-conversion.

_____

## Guardrail + read date

- **Guardrail:** _	Support ticket volume (watch for notification fatigue/annoyance), unsubscribe/notification opt-out rate
- **Read date:** _Whenever we have accumulated sufficient sample size at 80% power / 95% confidence for the expected effect size, with 3 weeks estimate of how long it will take to reach the sample size given typical trial volume
