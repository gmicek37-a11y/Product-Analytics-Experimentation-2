## The mechanic

_A Progress Bar
_____

## Rationale

_The endowed-progress ledger exploits the goal-gradient effect. Motivation to finish something increases as the perceived distance to the finish line shrinks — that's why starting the ledger pre-credited at 25% (rather than 0%) matters: it's not decorative, it changes how far the remaining work feels, even though the actual number of steps is identical. This is a well-documented effect (people are measurably more likely to finish a loyalty card that starts "2 of 10 stamps already given" than an equivalent "0 of 8"), and it's specifically aimed at the moment right before data connection — the highest-friction, highest-drop-off point in the flow.
